### Offline Website Preview

In the VSCode PowerShell terminal, run

1. cd "<LOCAL PATH>"
2. $env:Path = "C:\Ruby40-x64\bin;$env:Path"
3. jekyll serve --livereload

Then open 'http://localhost:4000' in a browser or VSCode
